RoboRacer Raceline Comparison

8 global racelines, each driven by the ForzaETH race stack for 10 timed laps on the AutoDRIVE RoboRacer practice track.

Setup

Simulator
AutoDRIVE RoboRacer 2026-iros (practice)
Track
autodrive_roboracer, ~28 m loop, 1.06–2.46 m wide
Planner / controller
ForzaETH race stack (ros2-humble), MAP controller
Speed scaling
0.5× each line’s own speed profile
Laps per line
1 standing start + 10 timed flying laps
Safety margin
0.25 m from line centre to each wall
  • Planner. ForzaETH in time-trial mode. Its state machine stays in GB_TRACK and feeds the imported global line to the MAP controller (SIM_linear lookup table), which produces every steering and speed command. In time trials there is no online replanning, so each run tests the global line plus ForzaETH’s tracking.
  • Why 0.5× speed. The speed profiles were computed by the TUM optimizer for a generic F1Tenth car (8 m/s top speed, 10 m/s² lateral grip). That model was never measured against this simulated car. At 0.8×, ForzaETH’s own line crashed on 5 of 5 laps; at 0.5× every line except the raw IL line ran clean. The same 0.5× is used for every line so the comparison is fair. Top driven speed was about 3.7 m/s.
  • Safety margin. All TUM lines were optimized with width_opt = 0.50 m, which keeps the line centre at least 0.25 m from each wall. With a 0.31 m wide car that leaves about 0.10 m clearance per side. The centerline sits ≥ 0.48 m from the walls and needs none. The IL line came with no margin, so a second version, il_line_margin, was made to match: points were pushed back from the walls before TUM smoothing so the final line has 0.26 m.
  • Timing and crashes. Lap times use the simulator’s own clock. Statistics cover the 10 flying laps; the standing first lap is excluded. A crash is the simulator’s collision counter firing, or the car stuck for 1.5 s. Each crash resets the car to the start line and that lap is not counted.

  • Min time is fastest: 12.28 s mean, 0.21 s per lap ahead of min_curvature. The starting guess (centerline, min curvature or IL line) made no difference.
  • The IL line with margin is 0.20 s slower than min time, about level with min_curvature, and keeps the most room to the wall of the fast lines (0.10 m driven).
  • The raw IL line crashed on all 10 attempts at the same spot, the S-bend near (−1.26, −2.15), about 6.1 s into the lap at 2.8 m/s. It passes 0.16 m from the wall, which leaves under 1 cm for the car’s edge.
  • Shortest is slowest: min_distance is 1.1 m shorter than min_curvature but 2.8 s slower, because its tighter corners force a much lower speed profile.
  • Centerline is the safest (0.29 m from the wall) and 0.67 s slower than min time.
